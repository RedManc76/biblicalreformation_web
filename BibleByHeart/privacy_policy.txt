Bible Memory Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Bible Memory ("the app") is designed to help you memorise Scripture. Your privacy matters to us.

Data stored on your device
The app stores your memorisation progress, review schedules, settings, and voice recordings locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Microphone
The app uses the microphone only when you choose to record passages. Recordings are saved on your device and are not uploaded by the app.

Network use
If you select the ESV translation, the app fetches Bible text from the ESV API (api.esv.org). No personal information is sent with these requests beyond the passage reference.

Backup and restore
You may export or import a backup zip file. Backups contain your local data and recordings. You control where backup files are stored.

Analytics
The app does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developer through the App Store or Play Store listing.

Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
